# Business Case

## Title

Credit Risk & Loan Default Analysis  
Lending Club Portfolio Risk, Vintage Performance, Expected Loss & Default Probability Analysis

## Business problem

Lending institutions need to balance portfolio growth with credit risk. Management requires evidence-based analysis to identify high-risk borrower and loan segments, understand how default risk changes over time, estimate potential losses, and determine where underwriting, pricing, monitoring or portfolio-management actions should be considered.

The question this project answers is:

**Where is credit risk concentrated, why is it occurring, how much potential loss does it represent, and what actions should the business take?**

## Why the problem matters

A book can grow while risk-adjusted performance deteriorates. Default rates, expected-loss dollars and vintage development can move in different directions. A small high-rate niche may be less important than a large mid-risk segment that dominates funded exposure.

## North Star Metric

**Risk-adjusted portfolio performance**, expressed as expected loss relative to exposure.

Primary metrics:

- Expected Loss $
- Expected Loss %
- Default Rate on matured loans
- Charged-off rate
- Probability of Default
- Exposure at risk

Secondary metrics:

- Funded amount
- Average loan amount
- Interest rate
- Recovery rate
- Net loss rate
- Loan count

Expected loss is the North Star because it combines likelihood, severity and exposure. A segment with a high default rate and tiny balances is not the same management problem as a slightly lower-rate segment with very large funded exposure.

## Scope

In scope:

- Accepted Lending Club loans from 2007 to 2018 Q4
- Portfolio composition
- Credit-risk segmentation
- Vintage performance
- Analytical expected loss
- Origination-time probability-of-default model
- Tableau-ready decision views

Out of scope:

- Automated approve/decline decisions
- Use of protected characteristics for credit policy
- Claims of causal effect from a predictive model
- Official regulatory capital calculations

## Decision the analysis supports

1. Whether underwriting should be tightened for specific segments
2. Whether risk-based pricing should be reviewed
3. Which segments need enhanced monitoring
4. Which investigations should be first in the queue

## Success

The work is successful if a risk or lending stakeholder can see the concentration of risk, the size of the exposure, the recommended action, the owner and the metric that will show whether the action worked.
