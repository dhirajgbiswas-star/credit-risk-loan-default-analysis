# Stakeholder Management

## Stakeholder matrix

| Stakeholder | Interest | Decision | Information required |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chief Risk Officer | Portfolio risk | Risk appetite | Expected loss, PD, concentration |
| Head of Credit | Underwriting | Policy changes | Segment default rates and evidence |
| Portfolio Manager | Portfolio performance | Allocation and monitoring | Vintage, exposure, EL dollars |
| Product Manager | Product design | Pricing and product rules | Risk-return by purpose and term |
| Risk Analyst | Monitoring | Investigation queue | KPIs and exception segments |
| Data Team | Data reliability | Pipeline and quality rules | Completeness, validity, lineage |
| Compliance | Regulatory risk | Governance | Fairness, purpose limitation, controls |
| Executive Team | Business performance | Strategic priorities | Executive KPIs and actions |

## Communication principles

- Lead with the business question, then the evidence, then the action.
- Separate observed rates from estimated or assumed quantities.
- Do not present a high default rate as the same problem as a high expected-loss dollar amount.
- State sample size and confidence when a segment is thin.
- Do not use model language that implies causation.
- Assign an owner and a metric to every recommendation.
- Keep sensitive or proxy variables out of individual-level decision rules.
